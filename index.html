Article IMission & Purpose

Three commitments, one charter.

We are organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes — built to move as quickly as the fields we study, and to stay accountable to the people we serve.

§ 1

Legal Exchange

Convening research, dialogue, and cross-border exchange in international arbitration, energy and environmental law, trade and export control, and AI governance.

§ 2

Professional Development

Training and mentoring young legal professionals and scholars entering fields that most law schools have not yet caught up to.

§ 3

Pro Bono Legal Aid

Providing free legal aid services to low-income individuals — the same rigor, applied where it is needed most.

Emerging law does not wait for legal education to catch up. Our work is to close that gap — in the classroom, in practice, and in the communities that need representation most.

— Founding Charter, International Frontier Legal Network
Article IIFields of Practice

Four frontiers, studied in the open.

The areas of law that will decide how the next decade of cross-border commerce, energy, and technology actually works — and where young practitioners have the least formal training.

01 International Arbitration Cross-border dispute resolution and the institutions that govern it.
02 Energy & Environmental Law The legal architecture of the energy transition.
03 Trade & Export Control Sanctions, export regimes, and the compliance frameworks around them.
04 AI Governance Emerging regulatory frameworks for artificial intelligence.

Article IVHow We Work

From first exchange to standing counsel.

A working method as deliberate as the law we study.

i.

Convene

Bring together practitioners, scholars, and fellows around a defined frontier-law question.

ii.

Train

Mentor young legal professionals through structured fellowships and working groups.

iii.

Publish

Turn research and exchange into resources the field can actually use.

iv.

Serve

Apply that same expertise directly, through free legal aid to those who need it.
