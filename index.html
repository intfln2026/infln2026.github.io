About Us

The International Frontier Law Network (IFLN) is an independent nonprofit organization committed to advancing the rule of law, international cooperation, and evidence-based public policy. We bring together legal professionals, scholars, policymakers, researchers, and industry leaders to address the legal and regulatory challenges created by emerging technologies and global transformation.

Our Mission

International Cooperation

Strengthen dialogue and collaboration among governments, international organizations, universities, and civil society.

Research & Policy

Promote interdisciplinary research on emerging technologies, AI governance, digital regulation, environmental sustainability, and international dispute resolution.

Public Interest

Support public-interest initiatives that advance justice, human dignity, innovation, and sustainable development.

Research Focus

Artificial Intelligence Governance

Legal and regulatory frameworks for trustworthy, transparent, and accountable AI.

International Economic Law

Cross-border trade, investment, dispute resolution, and international arbitration.

Environmental & Climate Law

Climate governance, carbon markets, sustainability, and green development.

Digital Society

Privacy, cybersecurity, digital rights, platform governance, and emerging technologies.

Public Policy

Evidence-based policymaking supporting innovation, inclusion, and global cooperation.

Rule of Law

Judicial cooperation, legal reform, access to justice, and institutional development.
